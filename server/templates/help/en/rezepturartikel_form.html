What a prepared item is

Prepared items exist only in this app. The result holds exclusively the articles they resolve into through their recipe. The unit decides how counting works: in piece(s) they are counted off, in g the whole container goes onto the scale. That is what the containers are for, whose tare is deducted.

Recipe

The quantity applies per one unit of this prepared item and is given in the base unit of the respective product. A portion of nachos consists, for instance, of 1 small nacho box and 0.140 kg of nachos.

Shares in percent

A weighed mixture is easier to describe as shares of the weight: 40 % of one product, 35 % of the second, 25 % of the third, exactly 100 together. That is what the box is for. The app converts into the product's unit: for products in g or kg by itself, for products in litres or pieces through the weight of one unit stored on the product. If that is missing, the recipe says so on the row. Water or anything else that is not a product does not appear in the recipe. The shares only describe what is counted.
