Roles

Counters only use the counting app. Managers may also use this web interface: maintaining master data, creating and closing inventories. Administrators additionally reach the administration and the backups. An administrator is created by an administrator in the administration, which has the box for it. An administrator is always a manager too.

Sites

The sites of a counter are a suggestion: for an inventory of these sites they appear at the top of the “Counters” page, and when creating one their site stands next to the name. They are still entered there explicitly. For managers the selection is hidden, because they are not tied to a site. Their sites are not lost in the process.

Language

The language applies to the person's interface{% if mail_moeglich %} and to their e-mails{% endif %}. When creating, your own is preselected. The person can change it later in the account menu.

Invitation or password

A new account is invited: the person gets an e-mail with a link, sets their password there and is activated with it. That way nobody else knows their password. Until then the account shows as invited in the list, and on its page the invitation can be sent again, say when the link has expired. It lasts {{ einladung_tage }} day{{ einladung_tage|pluralize }}. Whoever would rather give the password right away ticks Set the password yourself. Then the two fields appear.

Whoever switches an invited account on and deactivates it again makes every link sent so far invalid. The account stays invited, and a new invitation brings a link that works again.

First password

When creating the account you give the first password and pass it on to the person. They change it themselves afterwards.

Address

The address is changed by the person themselves, in the counting app through the account menu and on the website through the account menu or the block page, or by an administrator here and in the administration. A manager sees the field greyed out only.{% if mail_moeglich %} A new address first gets a mail with a link. The address applies once the person has confirmed it, until then the previous one stays. That way a mistyped address never gets into the account. Only an invited or deactivated account gets the new address at once: there is nothing to confirm there, and the invitation should go to the right address. Only the last link counts. A new password, a removed passkey or deactivating takes it back.{% endif %}

Password

The password is not changed here. Everyone changes their own: managers through the menu at the top right, counters in the counting app through the account menu or through the links on the block page of the website. It is stored only as a check value anyway. Nobody can read it out, not even an administrator. Whoever forgets theirs gets a new one set by an administrator in the administration. A manager cannot do that.{% if mail_moeglich %} “Forgot your password?” on the sign-in page works too.{% endif %} A new password signs the account out on every device, in the counting app too. Only whoever changes their own stays signed in on the device they do it on. What has not been sent on the others yet stays and goes out after the next sign-in.

{% if mail_moeglich %}

Notifications

Whoever changes, deactivates or deletes an account sends the person an e-mail. It names what changed, with the old and the new value, and who did it. When the address changes, it goes to the old one and to the new one. Likewise everyone gets a mail when their password is set anew or a passkey is added or removed, even when they did it themselves. A deactivated account only gets the mail about the deactivation. Only once it is active again do mails come again.

What the mails say is set by an administrator in the administration under Mail texts. That is also where it is decided whether a mail names who triggered it.

{% endif %}

When someone leaves

Whoever leaves the business has their account deleted, not set to inactive. No count data hangs on the account. Only the assignments to sites and inventories and the passkeys disappear. An account is created again in a minute, should somebody come back after all. Inactive is meant for a temporary case, such as a longer absence. Whoever sets an account to inactive signs it out everywhere, on the website and in the counting app. Once it is active again, the person signs in anew. What their counting app has not sent yet stays and goes out after the new sign-in.
