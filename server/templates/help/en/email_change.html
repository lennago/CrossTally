A new address

Here you change the e-mail address of your account.{% if bestaetigung %} First a mail with a link goes to the new address. Only once you have confirmed it does the address apply. Until then the old one stays, and a typo does not cut you off from your mails.{% else %} The new address applies at once.{% endif %} In the counting app it works the same way, through the account menu.

The link

It lasts {{ tage }} day{{ tage|pluralize }} and works once. If you enter another address before that, only the new link counts. A new password takes it back, and so do a removed passkey and a deactivated account. Whoever had your device or your password no longer gets at the address that way. Then simply enter it once more.

Password or passkey

Before that the page asks for your password. A session left open is not enough on its own, because whoever changes the address could take over the account.{% if mail_moeglich %} With the new address and “Forgot your password?” that would be easy.{% endif %} A wrong password counts like a failed attempt at signing in.

If your account has no password, you confirm with one of your passkeys instead. After Confirm with passkey the device asks for face, finger or PIN. Then you have two minutes to send the address. Without a password and without a passkey there is nothing to confirm with. Then an administrator can set a password for you.
