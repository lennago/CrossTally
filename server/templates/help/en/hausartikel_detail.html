Containers when weighing

The assignment belongs to the product and therefore applies at every site. It is shown here because you notice what something is weighed in while maintaining a site. “No container” (scale tared) is always offered when counting anyway. Nothing is preselected: the counters choose the container in every weighing line themselves, because a guessed tare would go unnoticed while counting.

Packs

The packs belong to the product, and so does their order. It can be changed here: drag by the handle or move with the arrows. The arrows save at once, after dragging “Save order” saves. That is how the packs then appear when counting, at every location and at every site that carries the product. An inactive pack is no longer offered when counting and gets no line in new inventories, even where a count list names it. New packs are created under “Edit”, quantities and names are changed on the product's page.

Occurs at

Where the article occurs is decided by the count list of the location. The order and the packs per location are kept there. “Add” ticks all active packs there. “Remove” only takes it out of the count list. The article itself remains.
