Locations

Areas and locations of the site are set up on its detail page after it has been created.

Counters

Here you record who usually counts at this site. That is a suggestion: for an inventory of this site these people appear at the top of the “Counters” page, and when creating one their site stands next to the name. They are still entered there explicitly.

Managers are not on offer. They are assigned per inventory individually. Administrators may count everywhere anyway.
