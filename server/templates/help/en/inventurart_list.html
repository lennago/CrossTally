What inventory types are for

An inventory type is a tab of the counting app, say the monthly, the annual or the cup inventory. Every article belongs to exactly one. When an inventory is created, the types it counts are chosen. No type counts another one along: an annual inventory is an inventory with all types.

The order

This is how the tabs stand in the counting app, the sections of the count lists, the types when creating, and the name of an inventory. The arrows move a type by one place. The first one is preselected wherever nothing else applies: when creating an inventory and for a new product.

The columns

  • Active articles per site says where an inventory of this type is worth it. A site without active articles of the type cannot be chosen for it alone.
  • Articles leads to the product list with the products of this type.
  • Inventories counts how many inventories count the type.

Articles are assigned on the product (for all its articles), on the article or for a whole article group on its page.

Deleting

A type can be deleted as long as no inventory counts it, because their name carries it. Its articles then move to another type, which the delete page asks for. In the count lists they then stand at the end of the new type. The last type always stays: without it an article would have none.
