The three steps

  • Preparation: the count lines exist. Now is the time for the target stock, from the file or by hand. A missing inventory type is added through “Change types”. “Start counting” releases the inventory to the counting app.
  • Counting: the counters fill the lines in the counting app. Here you can follow what has arrived. Nothing is entered on this page on purpose. Differences, notes and closing are under “Review”. Once everything is counted, “Lock” stops the counting.
  • Closed: the lines are frozen and do not change any more, not even if a target stock later turns out to be wrong. In the merchandise management system they are entered by hand.

Locking

Once counting is done, Lock stops it. In the counting app only the review list stays open: notes and the difference by hand go on, the server takes no new counts. Whatever a phone still sends from a dead spot waits there until counting is open again. The lock on the button shows the state, Open releases counting again. Managers switch it here and in the app. Closing works locked or open, and “Back to preparation” opens the lock.

Importing the target stock

First choose the system the file comes from. For WinTICKET that is the inventory count list as an Excel file. Its menus are German: Berichte → Warenwirtschaft → Zähl-, Auffüll- und Differenzlisten → Inventurzählliste mit Sollbestand nach Lager und Artikelnummer (236), saved as “Excel-Datei ohne Formatierung” (Excel file without formatting). Read are the article number, the name and “Istbestand lt. Rechner” (stock according to the system). The header and warehouse rows of the sheet do no harm. A CSV file with “article number;quantity” works too, and a column with the name may be there too. Taken over is what {{ inventur.haus.name }} carries. Unknown numbers, discontinued articles with stock and inactive ones with stock that are due at this inventory become a question under “Clarify target stock”. The target stock of articles of other types is stored and only counts once someone counts them. The stock of a discontinued duplicate goes to the article it counts as. Articles with the status “Not countable” are skipped.

Without a file, or for the articles it does not know, there is “Maintain by hand”: the target per article for entering. It counts like one from the file.

The last file counts: whatever it does not name falls away, a target by hand included. Importing only happens during preparation. If something is missing later: “Back to preparation”, upload, start again. The counted quantities are kept in the process. On closing, the target stocks disappear. What remains is their copy in the result.

Difference by hand

If there is no file with the stock, but the merchandise management system shows the difference once the counted quantities are in there, it can be entered here: Enter the difference by hand in the card “Target stock”. That only works as long as no target stock came in, neither from a file nor by hand. With a target the difference computes itself, and two differences per article would contradict each other.

It is entered per article in the review list or in the counting app, there by the counters too. Managers switch it on and off here, above the review list and in the app. As long as it is on, no target stock comes in. Switched off, what has been entered stays and applies again once it is switched on again. On closing, the entered difference is frozen, and the target stays empty.

Counters

Only those assigned to the inventory see it in the counting app. Administrators may count everywhere anyway. Closing deletes the assignment: who counted is not kept.

Taking back and deleting

“Back to preparation” only turns the status around. Counted quantities stay. Deleting takes count lines, target stocks and notes with it. A closed inventory cannot be deleted here, because the only record of the day hangs on it. That is only possible in the administration.
