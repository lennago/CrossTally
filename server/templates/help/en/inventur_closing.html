No longer active, but counted

Whoever finds an inactive or discontinued article on the shelf can still count it in the counting app. Here it can be reactivated. For a discontinued one that only works as long as no other article has taken over its number or its product. Otherwise the reason is given in the line. A discontinued article gets its places in the count lists back at their old position in the process, as far as it remembered them. The counted quantity goes into the result in any case.

Counted unexpectedly

Whatever was added in the counting app through the search and carries a quantity can go into the location's count list: the article together with its pack, only the pack if the article is already there, or the weighing. Whatever is added goes to the end of the list. A line left empty is no find, and a discontinued article is expected nowhere any more. Neither stands here.

Expected, but nothing entered

A pack from the count list nobody entered anything into, or that was deleted in the app. Only this pack at this location is removed. The article stays with its other packs. The scale stands here only if the article also has an active pack there. If it only has the scale there, it is removed in the count list of the location. If the article was not counted anywhere, it also appears under “Not counted at all”.

Not counted at all

Listed here is everything nothing was entered for and where “Target counts” is not set. Inactive means: not in the range right now, will return. The article stays in the count lists but gets no line any more until it is active again. Discontinue means: over. It drops out of every count list and frees up number and product. Both only concern the record at {{ inventur.haus.name }}. Switch to … puts the article into another inventory type, say the annual inventory if it should be counted only once a year. If the product has further articles, the same question comes as on the article's page: this article only, or all articles of the product, at the other sites too.

Counted, but without a target stock

These articles were counted but have no target, neither from the file nor by hand. Without a target stock the line freezes without a difference. The find would look as if it fitted. Target 0 means: the merchandise management system holds no stock here, everything counted is surplus. An own value is given in the unit {{ inventur.haus.name }} carries the article in. The unit stands behind the field. The value may be negative. The choice only takes effect on closing and only for this inventory. Until then it can be taken back here.

Counted, but in no type of this inventory

These articles belong to none of the types this inventory counts ({{ inventur.arten_anzeige }}), but were counted in the app anyway. The quantity goes into the result either way. Take into … puts the article into one of the types of this inventory. From the next inventory of that type on it then gets its lines. Here too the page asks whether that applies to this article only or to all articles of the product. Listed is only what was counted itself, not what came along as an ingredient of a prepared item.

Already decided in the run

If another site of the same day chose a type for all articles of a product while closing, that is noted on the line. For the whole product it cannot be turned around here, only for this article. Whoever wants it otherwise changes it afterwards on the product itself.

Weighed in a container from elsewhere

When weighing, the counting app offers the containers of other sites last. If one of those was used, the assignment is evidently wrong: the container belongs to this article, or it also exists at this site. With an answer it appears further forward next time. The weighing itself counts in any case.

Weighed at 0 g

A 0 on the scale means: there was nothing to weigh here. It counts as counted, with a quantity of zero, and raises no question in any block. The scale is not there to be taken out, and the chosen container does not count as one from elsewhere.
