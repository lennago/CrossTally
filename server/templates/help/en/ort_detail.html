What “expected” means

The expectation drives the completeness check and the order of the count list. Everything may still be counted. Unexpected articles are marked as such while counting. Which articles appear here, their order and their packs: all of that is in the count list. “Edit location” is only for name and area. The other way round, a location can also be assigned from the detail page of an article.

A location without expectations

A location at which nothing is expected appears in the counting app too. There it carries “nothing expected” instead of a progress. What stands there after all is added through “Add article”.
