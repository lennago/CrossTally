Name and status of the group

The name comes from the merchandise management system and is what the import recognises it by. Changing it creates a second group at the next import. The group has no status of its own: it stands as its liveliest article does. The buttons above therefore set all of its articles, at every site. Individual ones can be set differently again afterwards in their row.

Inventory type

The selection above sets the inventory type for all articles of this group, at every site. It does not filter, which is why it asks first. If the articles are in different types, it shows mixed. The group hangs on the article, and so does the choice here: articles of the same product in other groups stay as they are.

The header row of each product has the same selection for the product. It applies to all its articles, including those in other groups and at other sites.

Status of the products

The status of a product acts on all of its articles, not only those from this group. The rows below carry their own status: active, inactive or discontinued. Whatever hangs under “Not countable” does not appear here. The column “Weighing” applies to the whole product as well: “No” also removes its containers.
