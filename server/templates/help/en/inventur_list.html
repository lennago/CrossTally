Inventories and runs

An inventory belongs to a site. If several sites count on the same day, they stand here together as one block. The cut-off date connects them, and the header row names every type counted in this run. A click on the header row opens the run: all sites side by side, with a shared closing.

The three steps

  • Preparation: the count lines exist, the target stock is imported or maintained by hand. The inventory is not yet visible in the counting app.
  • Counting: released with “Start counting”. Counting happens exclusively in the counting app. These pages show progress and differences.
  • Closed: the results are frozen and do not change any more. There is also the sheet as a PDF.
