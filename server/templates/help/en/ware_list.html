What a product is

The same product across all sites. Base unit and packs hang on it. They apply everywhere. What a single site carries of it (number, name, article group, inventory type) sits on the article below it. The column “Inventory type” shows the type of the articles and changes it for all of them at once.

Searching and filtering

The search covers name, article number, EAN, article group and product family. Typing the family above also finds the products in the families below it. A typo does no harm: when the search finds nothing, it shows what sounds alike. Numbers have to be exact.

Product family and article group are multiple selections and only take effect with “Apply”. Under “Carried at”, a click per site switches between any, must and not. If nothing is set to must, one of the other sites is enough. The filter “Inventory type” finds a mixed product under each of its types, and “Mixed” shows only those.

The column “Weighing” switches weighing for the product at every site. If the weight of one unit is still missing for it, the message says so. “No” also removes the product's containers.

What the merchandise management system carries as an article without it being a product lies in the product family Not countable and only appears here when that family is selected in the filter.
