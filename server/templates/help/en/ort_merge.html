What happens when merging

On offer are the locations in the area {{ ort.bereich.name }}. {{ ort.name }} remains, the selected location disappears. Its articles are appended to the count list, in their previous order. If an article already sits at both locations, it stays where it is and only gains the packs it is still missing here. If either location expected weighing, the joint one does.

Name and timing

The remaining location may take a new name, also that of the vanishing one. If count lines of an open inventory still hang on the vanishing location, merging is possible only once that inventory is closed or deleted.
