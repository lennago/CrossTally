Weighing

Weighing is gross in grams. The tare of the chosen container is deducted. The net quantity is then spread over the recipe onto the products involved.

Resolvable

Resolvable means: every component has a carried article at this site. If an ingredient is inactive there, the prepared item stands like an inactive article: it can be put into the count list and added in the app, but gets no line of its own. If an ingredient is missing or discontinued, the item is not on offer there.

Occurs at

Where a component is missing, the item cannot be resolved and therefore cannot be entered either. A location already entered can still be removed. The order per location is kept in its count list. A prepared item has no packs: it is counted in its own unit, one line per location.
