Inactive, but with a target stock

The merchandise management system holds a stock, here the article is not in the range right now. Activate again takes the target stock over and creates its count lines wherever it is expected. Discard leaves the article inactive and drops the target stock. If the file lists the article with a stock of 0, nobody is asked: the merchandise management system and the site agree, and the target stock is saved silently.

Discard all

Discarding also works for a whole block and, at the bottom of the page, for all open lines together. It changes nothing in the master data, only the target stock of these lines is dropped. Useful when the file is going to be uploaded again anyway or the questions have already been answered elsewhere. The only way back is a new upload.

Discontinued, but with a target stock

The number belongs to a discontinued record. Either the article comes back (Activate again: only possible as long as number and product are free at the site, and brings its places in the count lists back to their old position, as far as it remembered them), or the merchandise management system has reassigned the number: then create the new article and afterwards press “Match again” at the bottom of the page. Here too: with a stock of 0 in the file, the question does not arise.

If the site now actively carries the same product under another number, the line names that article. Then the merchandise management system gave the product a new number and still holds stock on the old one. One product is counted, so the target stock is the sum: Add to … adds it to the active article's target stock, in that article's unit. The old number then counts as the new one, like a duplicate after merging. On the next import its stock is added by itself, and the article import no longer offers it for bringing back. “Activate again” does not work here, because the product is taken.

Number unknown

{{ inventur.haus.name }} does not know these numbers. If it is an article that is missing here: Create new opens the form with number and name and leads back here after creating. Then press “Match again” at the bottom, and the target stock lands in its place. The file does not have to be uploaded again for that.

Name differs

The number fits, the name in the file does not. Usually a rename in the merchandise management system, but possibly a reassigned number, and then the old record should be discontinued and the new one created. The target stock sits on the article here either way.

Active, but without a target stock

{{ inventur.haus.name }} carries these articles as active, and they have no target stock, neither from the file nor by hand. Inactive means: it will return, it stays in the count lists. Discontinue means: over, number and product become free. In both cases the still empty count lines of this inventory disappear. Whoever wants the article counted anyway leaves it be.

Articles of other types in the file

The file usually names the whole stock, including articles that belong to none of the types of this inventory ({{ inventur.arten_anzeige }}). Their target stock is saved but does not count here and does not appear in the review list. If somebody counts such an article in the app anyway, its target stock and difference are ready. The same holds for an inactive article that is still listed as a question above.
