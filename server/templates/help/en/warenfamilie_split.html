What happens when splitting

A second family is created beside {{ familie.anzeigename }}. A click on a product moves it to the other side. Both halves inherit the lead unit from the previous family. If one half afterwards carries only one dimension and no longer the previous lead unit at all, it totals in the unit of its first product. If no product of the family carried the lead unit before, it was chosen on purpose, say litres over bottles in pieces. Then it stays in both halves, as long as every product has its conversion to it. The conversions hang on the products and move with them. Subfamilies stay with the previous family. A family in portions keeps the portion.
