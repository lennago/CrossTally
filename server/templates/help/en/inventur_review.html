What the review list is for

Here you see what closing freezes: per article the counted actual across all locations, the target stock and the difference. The whole scope is listed, including what has a target stock but has not been counted yet. Prepared items are already spread over their products through their recipe.

Every difference carries its sign. +12 means there is more than expected. -12 means something is missing. The colour says the same: green means it matches. Yellow means there is more. Red means something is missing. Both go for the sums as well, only there the colour is quieter. A sum is an overview and not a finding. How much colour you see is in your account menu under “Colours of the difference”.

By product family

Under “View” the list can be grouped by product family. In the header of each family the total difference then appears: all differences of its articles added up, in the lead unit of the family, exactly as it appears in the report after closing. What is counted in is what is currently visible. A filter or a search therefore shrinks the sum as well. Articles without a difference contribute nothing. By article group there is no such sum, because an article group mixes articles in units that cannot be added up. Families within families form a tree: collapsed only the top ones, a click opens the subfamilies, and the articles stand in the family they belong to directly. Every family carries the total over everything below it.

Updating the values

While somebody is reading here, counting goes on. “Update values” fetches the new numbers without rebuilding the page. Notes that have been started stay. If articles turn up that are still missing from the list, a note says that a reload is due.

The numbers here only change through this button. Even while searching and filtering they stay as they were fetched. The page checks every 30 seconds whether there is anything new. As long as there is not, the button is disabled and says No new values. If something was entered in the counting app, it turns yellow and reads New values · update. Whatever changes with it is marked in colour, and next to the timestamp it says how many articles there were. After a page reload nothing is marked any more.

As long as there are new values, closing is not possible. It would otherwise close with numbers nobody here has seen. Update and review first, then close. If the sign-in has expired, the button says Sign-in expired, and closing is not possible then either. After signing in again, say in another tab, the page notices it at its next check.

Reviewing is calmer when locked: “Lock” on the page of the inventory stops the counting, and the numbers stay put. Notes and differences go on, here as in the counting app.

While searching

The search covers number, name, product and article group. The hits are ordered by how well they match: first the articles whose product or name begins with the term, then those that contain it there, then those with a matching number, and last those that only match through their article group. Grouped, they stand in their groups by number. A click on a column header sorts by that column instead, and it stays that way until the filter is reset.

The view stays

The review list always comes back the way you last had it: search, filter, sorting and grouping, the expanded groups and the point you had scrolled to. That also holds when you come via “Review” on the page of the inventory and in a new tab. Reset filters forgets the view.

Note and “Target counts”

Notes save themselves when you leave the field. If the sign-in has expired meanwhile, the note stays in its field and the page says so, also when filtering and on “Update values”. After signing in again in another tab, “Save notes” here saves it. If someone else has entered something there meanwhile, in the counting app or in another tab, that stays, and the page names the article. Reloading shows what is there now. If the inventory has been closed meanwhile, it takes no more notes. Then too the text stays in its field, and the page says so. On closing the notes move into the result and appear on the PDF below the name of the product. The tick Target counts takes the target stock as the result on closing, for the case where a count was obviously off. It only takes effect on freezing. Without a target stock it comes to nothing.

Difference by hand

Without a target stock, the difference by hand can be switched on above the list, as on the inventory's page and in the counting app. Then a field for the difference from the merchandise management system stands instead of target and difference, in the unit of the actual. A minus means: something is missing. The field saves itself like a note. What is not a number is not saved, and the page says for which article. The counting app shows the same list, and whoever enters a difference there sees it here after reloading.

Closing

Closing happens from here or in the run through “Close all”. Whoever freezes should have seen what is being frozen. In the run the tab “Total” shows it. Before freezing, the application asks about what the count reveals about the master data. After that, count lines, target stocks and the assignment of the counters are gone. What remains are the result lines, with the notes in them.

If a note, “Target counts” or a difference by hand has been changed elsewhere in the meantime, for example in the counting app, the inventory is not closed. The page reloads and shows what is there now. What you enter here yourself does not stop the closing.
