What a prepared item is

Prepared products that exist only in this app: the assembled portion of nachos, the finished nut mixture. They are counted in piece(s), or they go onto the scale as a whole. On closing, the recipe resolves them into their products. The result holds only their articles.

Status

A prepared item has no status of its own. It stands as its ingredients do:

  • active as soon as all ingredients are active at one site.
  • inactive when one ingredient is inactive there. It can still be added while counting.
  • resolvable nowhere when an ingredient is discontinued or missing at every site. A prepared item without a recipe stands that way too.

The search also covers the ingredient: whoever types “nachos” sees the portions.
