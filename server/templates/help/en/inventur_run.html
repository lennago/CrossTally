A run

If several sites count on the same day, they belong to one run. Every site still has its own inventory with its own status, its own counters and its own target stock. The tabs above lead to them. “Total” shows all sites side by side.

Differences per product

Totalling happens over the product, not over the article number, because that differs from site to site. On the left are result and target stock across all sites, next to them the difference per site and on the far right the difference across all sites. An empty field means: the site does not carry the product. “-” means: there was neither a target stock nor a difference by hand.

Every difference carries its sign. +12 means there is more than expected. -12 means something is missing. The colour says the same: green means it matches. Yellow means there is more. Red means something is missing. Both go for the sums as well, only there the colour is quieter. A sum is an overview and not a finding. How much colour you see is in your account menu under “Colours of the difference”.

A product is opposing when one site is missing what another has too much of. In the sum the two cancel out. Usually a goods movement is behind it that was not booked in the merchandise management system.

While counting is still going on, open sites are calculated with their current state. Notes and “Target counts” are set in the review list of the site, not here.

Close all

This closes all open inventories of the run. It only works once every open site is counting. Sites without open questions are closed immediately. For the rest, the page “Before closing” comes up, one site after another. Whoever cancels there has closed part of the run. The rest can be closed later here again or in the tab of the site.

Only what you have seen here is frozen. If new values came from the counting app since the page was loaded, the button closes no further site, and the page shows the new numbers. Then press “Close all” again. Likewise when someone changed “Target counts” or a difference by hand. The same applies to a site that went on counting while another one was on the page “Before closing”. A new note does not stop the button, as notes do not show here.

If an inventory type is chosen for all articles of a product in the process, that applies at every site. At the other sites of the run it is then settled and can only be set differently there for the single article.

PDF and deleting

The sheet across all sites is available once they are all closed. It starts with the product families alone: each with its difference per site and across all sites, and the page its products are on. Every product follows, in the same columns as here. Every family at the top level closes with its total as a grey band.

The sheet of a single site is in its tab. “Delete open ones” deletes the inventories that are not closed yet. Closed ones stay.
