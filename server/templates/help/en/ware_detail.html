Packs and recipes stay in place in the process. “Not countable” puts the product into the family of the same name. That does not work as long as it sits in a recipe or one of its articles is counted in a running inventory. “Bring back” takes it out again: back into the old family, and the articles get their old status and their places in the count lists back at their old position, as far as family, location and packs still exist. Older records that did not yet remember family and places come back without them. If the old family now sums in a dimension the product has no conversion into, it stays where it is, and the message names the missing conversion.

Packs

The packs belong to the product, and so does their order: that is how they appear when counting, at every location and at every site. The arrows save at once, after dragging “Save order” saves. The × sets a pack inactive, the + brings it back. An inactive pack is no longer offered when counting and gets no line in new inventories. Quantities and names are changed under “Edit”.
