Two tabs

Select articles decides which articles occur at {{ ort.name }}. Count list decides order and packs. The way the goods sit on the shelf is the way they appear in the counting app.

Count list

The list is divided by inventory type, like the tabs of the counting app. Every type has its own section and within it its own order. Which type an article belongs to is set on the article, not in the list. A prepared item stands under the first type of its ingredients. If an article changes its type, it moves to the end of its new type at every location that expects it. An inventory without that type gives it no line. With only one inventory type there is no division.

Rows can be dragged by their handle, but only within their section. If you hold one at the top or bottom edge, the list scrolls along. The arrows move a row by one, the arrows with a bar move it all the way to the top or bottom of its section. Every change of order is saved immediately. One checkbox per pack: ticked, a line is created for it at the next inventory. At least one has to be ticked, otherwise the article would get no line here at all. For goods that can be weighed, weighing will do instead. The quantity beside it is the usual stock. It appears in grey in the empty field while counting, as a reference point, not as a preset.

An inactive pack stands in the row in grey with its default and “inactive”. New inventories give it no line. Whatever of it is still on the shelf is counted in an active pack, through the search if need be. The default stays and applies again as soon as the pack is active again.

Above the column the menu All rows acts on the whole list: tick or untick all packs, tick or untick only the loose single unit (“Single”) everywhere, tick or untick weighing everywhere. What would change nothing is greyed out. Unticking packs loses the quantities entered. If there are any, the entry asks first. This too is only saved with “Save”, or when switching to the Select articles tab: that saves what was changed in the list first.

Refresh at the top right fetches the list anew from the server without throwing away the ticks and quantities already entered here. That way a pack just created in another tab appears in its row.

The arrows and the × work even when a row is not in order, say a weighable article without pack and without weighing. The other rows are saved in the process. A changed row that is not in order reports it. Only “Save” insists on a list without errors.

For goods that can be weighed, the checkbox Weighing sits below: ticked, the scale is part of the count at this location too. If the goods only sit in their carton here, it stays empty. Otherwise a line would be left open at every inventory that nobody fills. Weighing is still possible in the counting app. Closing then asks whether weighing should be added here.

A newly added article comes without this tick. Only where a product has no pack at all is it set: the scale is then the only way to count it, and without the tick it would get no line.

If a pack is missing, it is created on the article. The link in the row leads straight to the “Packs” section of its edit page. A running inventory keeps its order. Changes apply from the next one.

Select articles

An article in the count list means: it is expected here. That drives the completeness check and the order while counting. Everything may still be counted. Unexpected items are marked as such while counting. In which order and in which packs is decided in the Count list tab. A newly added article arrives there with all its active packs ticked. Prepared items are only on offer where their recipe can be resolved. A discontinued article cannot be added here. Reactivate it first, on its page.
