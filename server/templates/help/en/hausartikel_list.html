Searching and grouping

The search covers number, name, EAN, product, article group and product family, for the family also the families below it. A typo does no harm: when the search finds nothing, it shows what sounds alike. Numbers have to be exact. Under “View” the list can be grouped by article group or product family. Otherwise a click on the column header sorts it.
