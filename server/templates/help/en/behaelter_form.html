Tare

The tare is the empty weight of the container and is always given in grams, no matter which unit the product is carried in, because the scale delivers grams. When counting it is deducted from the displayed weight.

Sites

There are two kinds of container. The spray cream bottle only ever holds spray cream. It belongs to its product and needs no site. The bowl, on the other hand, stands around at the site and holds whatever is being weighed right now: it gets the site or the sites where it exists.

When weighing, the counting app first offers what is assigned to the article, then the containers of its own site, and last those of other sites, in case one has been borrowed. A container without a site is only offered on its own articles. None is preselected: a guessed tare would go unnoticed while counting.

Assignment

On offer are the products that are weighed and the prepared items carried in g. Both can also be assigned from the other end: under “Edit” of the product or the prepared item, and on the page of one of the product's articles.

“No container” is offered at every weighing anyway and needs no assignment.
