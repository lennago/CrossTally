Total of the family

The total stands in the lead unit. Any unit works, a family in litres may hold nothing but bottles in pieces. Within one dimension the unit converts by itself (g ↔ kg, ml ↔ l). A product from another dimension brings its own conversion: that a bottle holds 500 ml or a portion weighs 120 g stands on the product's page. If it is missing there, the form asks for it when saving and writes the factor to the product.

Portions

Beside the units of the products the portion is always up for choice. No product is carried in portions, but a family can total in them: “Snacks” then counts bars and loose nuts as portions. For that every product says on its page what one portion is: “1 portion = 1 pcs” or “1 portion = 100 g”. If the entry is missing, the form asks for it.

Belongs to

A family can stand below another one. Its products are then counted there as well, in the unit of the family above. If “Beers” totals in pieces and “Bottled drinks” above it in litres, every beer needs its millilitres per bottle. Whatever is missing, the form asks for in one go.
