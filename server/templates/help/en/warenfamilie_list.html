What product families are for

Products that belong together, such as “all lemonades” or “all ice cream flavours”. The report and the run are grouped by them, also as PDF. The sheet of a single inventory is grouped by article group instead, like the merchandise management system. Unlike the article group, the family does not come from the merchandise management system but is maintained here.

Families within families

A family can belong to another one: “Bottled drinks” holds “Beers” and “Soft drinks”, and “Beers” holds the single brands. The list shows the tree: a subfamily stands indented under its family, and beside the number of products stands how many the family totals with its subfamilies. In the review list (view “By product family”), the report and the run the families form a tree: collapsed only the top ones, a click opens the subfamilies, and every family carries the total over everything below it, in its own unit. The PDFs show the same tree expanded.
