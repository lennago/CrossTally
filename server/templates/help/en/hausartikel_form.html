Source and number

The source is the system that carries the article. What a number looks like is up to the system. For WinTICKET it stands exactly as the file writes it: usually six digits, sometimes fewer, sometimes with a suffix such as “101014-1”. Own means: the article exists only here, for instance at a site without a merchandise management system. It has no number, and no system's file knows it. Its target stock is maintained by hand. Preselected is what the site already carries.

Article group

The category of the merchandise management system. It stays site-specific: the same product may well sit in a different group at another site, depending on whether it is bought centrally or locally.

Unit at the site

This is how the site carries the article, how the target stock arrives and how the result goes back. Almost always it is the unit of the product. Leaving it empty means exactly that. If the site counts in a different dimension than the product (pieces instead of kilograms, litres instead of pieces), the conversion to the product belongs with it: how many units of the product one unit of this site is. The field states the direction itself (“1 pcs = how many g?”). If the product is carried in g and the site counts a pack of 1 kg as a piece, 1000 goes there, not 0.001. Between g and kg or litres and millilitres the units convert by themselves.

A closed result keeps the unit the site counted in at the time. If an inventory that is not closed yet carries a target, an open target stock line or a difference by hand for the article, the unit stays until that inventory is closed. Those numbers are in the current unit.

Product

The identity of this article across all sites: the product. The packs hang on it and apply to all sites, and its unit is the one of the report. If another site already carries the same product, select it here. Otherwise describe a new one below. It is created in the unit of this site.

If the article changes its product, its closed results convert into the new one through the article. If one of these conversions would be too large to store, the article keeps its product, and the message names the result.

Inventory type

A new product goes into the first type of the list. {% endif %}

Settings of the product

When editing, the details of the product stand here too: its name, its family, whether it is weighed, the usual containers and the conversions. They belong to the product and not to the site. A change applies at every site that carries the product. The note above the fields names these sites.

Only what was changed is saved. Whoever selects another product above changes neither of the two: the settings disappear, because they belonged to the previous one. The unit of the product and its existing packs are changed on the product's page, because packs and recipes are converted in the process.

Packs

Counting is quantity × amount per pack: a crate of 20 bottles gets the 20, the single bottle gets the 1. Changing and reordering existing packs happens on the {% if object %} product. {% else %} product. {% endif %}

If the product already has a pack typed here, but inactive (same name or same quantity), the row is marked and asks: Activate again or Leave inactive. Nothing is saved before that.

Locations

The expectation drives the count list and the completeness check. The packs below it are the usual counting units at this location. A ticked location needs one of them, otherwise the article would get no line there. A pack that stays inactive does not count. Only goods that can be weighed may go without, they are then weighed there. A default quantity for them can be set later on the location.
