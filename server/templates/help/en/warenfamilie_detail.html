New subfamily

Creates a family below this one. Up for choice are first the products of this family, they move into the new subfamily, then all products without a family. The other subfamilies of this family can be moved under the new one as well. That is how “Drinks” splits step by step into “Beers” and “Soft drinks” and gains levels, without a detour past “No product family”.

The family “Not countable”

What the merchandise management system carries as an article without it being a product: deposits, vouchers, discounts. The articles of these products are not countable at every site: they appear in no list, no count and no report, and the import skips them silently. “Bring back” takes a product out: it returns to its old family, and its articles get their old status and their places back at their old position at every site, as far as family, location and packs still exist. Older records that did not yet remember family and places come back without them.
