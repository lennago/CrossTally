What happens when merging

{{ familie.anzeigename }} remains, the selected family disappears, and its products come here. Totalling still happens in the lead unit of {{ familie.anzeigename }}. Every product brings its own conversion into it. If one of the incoming products lacks it, the page asks for it before merging and stores it on the product. The same holds for the lead units of the families above, because they total these products too. If {{ familie.anzeigename }} has no lead unit yet, it takes over that of the vanishing family. Not countable is not on offer. Subfamilies of the vanishing family then stand under {{ familie.anzeigename }}.
