What an article group is

The category of the merchandise management system. It hangs on the article, not on the product, because the sites do not always file the same product in the same way, depending on whether it is bought centrally or locally.

The column “Inventory type” sets the type for all articles of the group. Articles of the same product in other groups stay as they are. The message says how many are affected.

Not countable

The button puts all products of the group into the family “Not countable”, all or nothing. The group then no longer appears in this list. Going back works per product through the product family.
