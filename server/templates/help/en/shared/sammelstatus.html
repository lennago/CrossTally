{# How product and article group get their status. Neither has one of its own. #}

Status

There is no status of its own here. It follows from the articles: active as soon as one is active, discontinued when all are discontinued, otherwise inactive. The status buttons therefore act on all articles that belong to it, at every site. Where an article cannot go back to active because its number or product is taken by something else in the meantime, the message says so.
