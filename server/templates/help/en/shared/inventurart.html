Inventory type

Every article belongs to exactly one inventory type, say the monthly, the annual or the cup inventory. An inventory counts the types chosen when it was created. In the counting app each of them becomes a tab.

On the product a new type applies to all its articles, at every site that carries it. On the article the application asks as soon as the product has further articles: this article only, or all articles of the product. If the articles of a product are in different types, it is called mixed. Its page then names the sites per type.

The count lists of the locations are divided by type. An article that changes its type then stands at the end of its new type at each of its locations.

Which types exist and in which order is set under Master data → Inventory types.
