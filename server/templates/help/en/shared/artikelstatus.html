{# The four article statuses: included wherever the status buttons are. #}

Status

  • Active: carried and counted.
  • Inactive: not in the range right now, but it will return (seasonal goods). The article keeps its number, its product and its place in the count lists. A new inventory creates no line for it. In the counting app it can still be added, provided the inventory counts its inventory type.
  • Discontinued: over. Delisted, or the number was reassigned in the merchandise management system. Number and product become free at the site, and the article drops out of every count list. Old inventory results still point to it. Delisted goods sometimes still stand on the shelf. That is why the counting app still offers it at the very end, as long as number and product are free at the site and the inventory counts its inventory type. Activated again, it gets its places in the count lists back at their old position, as far as location and packs still exist. An older record that did not yet remember its places when it was discontinued comes back without them. The article's page says which applies.
  • Not countable: never was a product, such as deposits, vouchers or discounts. The product moves into the family “Not countable”, disappears at every site from lists, counts and reports, and is silently skipped on import. Whoever puts a product there by mistake and takes it out again gets the old statuses back at every site. The product returns to its old family, and the articles get their places in the count lists back at their old position, as far as family, location and packs still exist. Older records that did not yet remember family and places come back without them.

By default the lists show what is carried: active and inactive.
